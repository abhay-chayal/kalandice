# Your Website Dashboard: a Quick Guide

Hi Kalandice! This guide shows you how to update your website yourself. Nothing here can
break the site. If you're ever unsure, save something as a **Draft** first, because drafts are
invisible to visitors.

**Dashboard address:** `https://encouragingpoetics.com/admin`

---

## Signing in for the first time

1. Look for an email titled **"You're invited to manage Encouraging Poetics"**. Check your spam folder too.
2. Click **Set your password**, choose a password, and type it twice.
3. You'll land on your dashboard. Bookmark this page!

**Forgot your password?** Go to the dashboard address, click **Forgot your password?**, and
we'll email you a link to choose a new one. Nobody (including your developer) ever needs
to know your password.

---

## Writing a blog post (Devotionals)

1. Click **Blog Posts → + New post**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**
   - **Category**: pick one or type a new one
   - **Scripture** (optional): shown in a highlighted box at the top
   - **Short summary**: one or two sentences shown on the Devotionals page
   - **Post**: your writing. Leave an **empty line between paragraphs**.
     - Start a line with `## ` to make a subheading
     - Start a line with `> ` to make a highlighted quote
   - **Cover image** (optional): click *Upload image* and choose a photo. Phone photos are fine.
3. Tick **Published** when you're ready for people to see it, or leave it unticked to save a draft.
4. Click **Save post**. It appears on your website within a few seconds.

To **edit** or **delete** a post, click it in the Blog Posts list. The delete button is at the bottom.

---

## Adding an event

1. Click **Events → + New event**.
2. Add the name, type of event, location, date and time exactly as you'd like visitors to read them,
   and a short description.
3. **RSVP link** (optional): paste an Eventbrite, Facebook or Zoom link if you have one.
4. **Calendar date** (optional): if you set it, the event disappears from your website by itself
   the day after it happens.
5. Click **Save event**.

---

## Updating your book

1. Click **Books** and then **Edit** on *Encouraging Poetics*.
2. You can change the description, themes (one per line), cover, purchase link and button text.
3. **Adding a new book later:** click **+ Add a book**. Choose **Coming soon** if it isn't out yet,
   and it will appear on the *More from Kalandice* shelf. Tick **Featured book** to make it the
   main book on your home page.

---

## Resources (playlists, support, church finder)

Click **Resources**. Each section has its own **+ Add** link.

To add a **Spotify playlist**: open the playlist in Spotify, tap **Share → Copy link**, and paste it in the Link box.

---

## Reader quotes

The "Encouragement Received" section on your home page shows kind words from readers.
It stays hidden until you add at least one, so nothing looks empty.

1. Click **Reader Quotes → + Add a quote**.
2. Paste what the reader wrote, add their name (a first name and last initial is plenty)
   and their city, then choose how many stars to show.
3. Click **Save quote**.

Please only add quotes from real readers who are happy for you to share them. A quick
"Would you mind if I put this on my website?" is all it takes.

---

## Newsletter signup wording

The signup box at the bottom of your home page is yours to word however you like.

1. Click **Newsletter Text**.
2. Change the heading, the description, the button, or the thank-you message.
3. Click **Save wording**.

A gentle suggestion: only promise what you'd enjoy keeping to. "Occasional words of
encouragement" is easier to live with than "every month" — and you can always change it later.

**Sending the newsletter itself:** go to **Subscribers** and click **Download spreadsheet
(CSV)**, then upload that file to a free service like MailerLite or Mailchimp and write your
letter there. They handle unsubscribe links and the rules around marketing email for you.

---

## Messages & prayer requests

Everything people send through your contact form, prayer box and speaking request form shows
up under **Messages**, and you'll also get an email. Prayer requests are private: only you can
see them.

- **Reply by email** opens a reply in your email app.
- **Mark read** keeps your list tidy.

---

## Newsletter subscribers

**Subscribers** lists everyone who signed up for your monthly letter. Click
**Download spreadsheet (CSV)** to get the list for Mailchimp, Flodesk or Gmail.
